# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Glyphs drawn the way the old picture books draw them: the middle sigil can sit in a ring of its own, and extra sigils sit in small circles inside lunes cut from the rim. A sigil in a lune mixes its element in, like a sigil beside the middle one.
- Petrification is redrawn after the glyph in the picture book: Earth in its own ring, four Eyes, four Earth seals in lunes.
- New spells: Floating Drops (Wind with Water), Rainflinger (Water with Fire) and Beast Repellent (Earth with Light), in the wind, waters and earth volumes.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Draw spells by hand: a sigil in the middle, signs around it, and nothing happens until the ring is closed.
- 38 spells to find in themed grimoires and on loose pages, plus a forbidden book.
- Spellbuilding: nesting of any depth, side sigils that mix elements, sign size as strength, chains of linked glyphs with Tread triggers (steps or redstone), glyph arrows and dispensers.
- A Witch's Grimoire of your own to draw in, name, annotate, copy into and copy out.
- Glyphs last while they are whole; rain and feet wear them gradually; Repetition mends them.
- 15 artifacts crafted dormant and woken with their glyph; sylph shoes for flight; the Void Basin and its world of stars.
- Inks, pens, Silverwood, library ruins.
- The Pact: forbidden magic, villagers who must not see magic made, Brimhats and the Knights Moralis.
- Create Aeronautics: fly a ship with a levitation glyph on its underside.
- EMI and JEI: recipes, info pages and an Awakening category.
