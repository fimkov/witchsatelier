# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Waymarked Windowways: signs drawn around a Window give it a mark, the name of a place. The first window with a mark is its Sill, its witch's second one is its Kin and opens both ways, and every other window with the mark is a Visitor that opens onto the Sill. Hold a page with another mark before any marked window and it turns, for a while, toward that mark's Sill, however far away and in whichever dimension.
- Someone else's Sill answers your page, and opens to the Visitors you draw with its mark, once you have stood by it or come out of it; a Sill in blood ink lets only its witch through; a Tread keeps a marked window shut; a Sill with no link of its own looks back the way you came for ten seconds.
- Arrivals are checked for room to stand, and a refused crossing is a thunk and a flicker, never text. Marked windows keep their roles, links and colours through restarts and are reached while their chunks are unloaded.
- Two new grimoire pages in Of Uncommon Signs, "Waymarks" and "A Page Held Up", and an advancement, Through a far window.
- Gamerules `witchsatelierWindowwaysAcrossDimensions` and `witchsatelierWindowwayDiscovery`, and the entity tag `witchsatelier:windowway_blocked` (the Ender Dragon, the Wither and the Warden by default).
- A window that turns someone away is a wall to them from both sides: whoever walks, falls or flies into it is set back out on the side they came from, in front or behind the wall, whole, never left half way in the hole or let through. This holds on floor and ceiling windows as on walls, and for riders: a mount or a boat carrying someone the window turns away is turned away with them, and a seat it cannot hold back drops its rider on the side they came from. Thrown things glance off either side.
- Grimoire pages that run long go on to the next spread, with a mark at the foot that the text continues and the title as a small running head; a cut never splits a word or leaves a line or two of a paragraph behind. Long titles wrap onto a second line instead of running off the page, and a contents list too long for one page is shown a page of rows at a time.
- Fixes: Windowways in unloaded chunks no longer tick.

## 1.2.1

- Windowways work with Sodium, Embeddium and Iris. With one of them installed the far side is drawn into the world itself, so the ground around a Windowway no longer tears open, and under a shader pack the far side is lit, shadowed and fogged by the pack like everything else: blocks, water and glass, creatures and chests, up to 30 blocks beyond the far disc.
- Glyph arrows can be shot from bows and crossbows, not only from dispensers.

## 1.2.0

- New spells from the manga: Pyreball Seal, Floatglow Lamp, Rising Platform of Water, Water Cage, Wall of Wind, Wall Bend, Water Horse, Ring of Fire, and the Seal of Obliviation.
- New signs: Region (where a spell may happen) and the Horse picture. A line struck through a sign switches it off; inward Columns lay the element flat; a Tread remembers who stood in it and can be turned inward.
- Petrification is redrawn with room to breathe: smaller lunes, eyes clear of them. Copies of it no longer read as a dancing puppet.
- New artifacts: Doorknob Doors, Guidance Orb, Fluttergram.
- The Pact: talebearers run for the bell, the Seal of Obliviation, Sinsinger's Talons, and petrified creatures wake fully only to blood ink.
- The Passage Stars meteor shower and the harvest after it; ten new advancements.
- Glyphs are read off the server thread, so drawing never stalls the game; drawings are thinned before reading.
- Lit glyphs, armed Tread traps and chains wake again after a restart.
- Fixes: a block duplication with rising earth, glyph arrows not casting on creatures, a statue renderer crash, oversized packets that could kick players, spells that ignored spawn protection and claims, spells ticking in unloaded chunks, temporary blocks left behind on quit, Tent Staff replacing blocks, Light Tracer Bangles never pairing, artifact blocks that dropped nothing, and many smaller ones.

## 1.1.0

- Glyphs drawn the way the old picture books draw them: the middle sigil can sit in a ring of its own, and extra sigils sit in small circles inside lunes cut from the rim. A sigil in a lune mixes its element in, like a sigil beside the middle one.
- Petrification is redrawn after the glyph in the picture book: Earth in its own ring, four Eyes, four Earth seals in lunes.
- New spells: Floating Drops (Wind with Water), Rainflinger (Water with Fire) and Beast Repellent (Earth with Light), in the wind, waters and earth volumes.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Draw spells by hand: a sigil in the middle, signs around it, and nothing happens until the ring is closed.
- 38 spells to find in themed grimoires and on loose pages, plus a forbidden book.
- Spellbuilding: nesting of any depth, side sigils that mix elements, sign size as strength, chains of linked glyphs with Tread triggers (steps or redstone), glyph arrows and dispensers.
- A Witch's Grimoire of your own to draw in, name, annotate, copy into and copy out.
- Glyphs last while they are whole; rain and feet wear them gradually; Repetition mends them.
- 15 artifacts crafted dormant and woken with their glyph; sylph shoes for flight; the Void Basin and its world of stars.
- Inks, pens, Silverwood, library ruins.
- The Pact: forbidden magic, villagers who must not see magic made, Brimhats and the Knights Moralis.
- Create Aeronautics: fly a ship with a levitation glyph on its underside.
- EMI and JEI: recipes, info pages and an Awakening category.
