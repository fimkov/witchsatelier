# Witch's Atelier

Draw your spells. A magic mod for Minecraft inspired by Witch Hat Atelier: ink a sigil, signs around it, close the ring.

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · [Wiki](https://fimkov.github.io/witchsatelier-wiki/)

## Features

**Drawing magic**
- Draw glyphs by hand on any block face (floors, walls, ceilings, across several blocks), on paper, in a Palm Quire, on boots and on artifacts.
- The spell wakes the moment the ring closes, and stops when the ring is broken, rubbed out or the block under it is gone.
- 8 sigils (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light, Crystal, Wind Underfoot, Aeriforms) and 25 signs; reversed signs give the opposite effect.
- How it is drawn matters: a bigger ring makes a bigger, stronger spell, a lopsided layout bends it, a careless hand misfires.
- 50 grimoire spells, from Pillar of Flame and Wall Breaker to Windowway portals, Birds of Light, a Water Horse, a Wall of Wind and a floating Pyreball that cooks your supper.
- A quick-close key for prepared pages and an imagined-ring technique.

**Spellbuilding**
- Rings inside rings, as deep as you like: every band adds its own layer to the spell at the heart.
- Sigils beside the middle one mix their elements in: Floating Drops, Rainflinger, Beast Repellent. Like the old books draw them, they sit in small circles in lunes cut from the rim, and the middle sigil can have a ring of its own.
- Big signs make a strong spell, small ones a weak one; a longer sign on one side pulls the spell that way.
- Region signs say where a spell may happen: inside the ring, outside it, on its line, or to one side.
- A straight line struck through a sign switches it off; Columns turned inward lay the element flat.
- A Tread glyph closed while someone stands in it answers only to them; turned inward, it works the other way round.
- Glyphs joined by lines are one chain: they light in a wave, or wait for a Tread glyph that works while it is stood on or powered by redstone: doors, traps, lights on a lever.
- Glyph arrows cast where they strike; dispensers shoot them, or cast a page's glyph from their own front.
- A Witch's Grimoire of your own: draw, name and annotate your glyphs, copy them from walls and old books, copy the whole book for a friend.

**Glyphs that last**
- Continuous spells work for as long as the glyph is whole.
- Rain washes ordinary ink and feet scuff glyphs on the floor; the ink thins, runs and smears before it breaks.
- Repetition keeps a glyph mended.

**Inks and tools**
- Conjuring, waterproof, invisible and lasting inks brewed from Silverwood, plus forbidden blood ink.
- Ink Pen and inkwell, Woodcruor Wand (chalk on stone), Searneedle (burns into metal), a rag to erase.
- Silverwood trees, and library ruins with themed grimoires, loose glyph pages and rarer finds.

**Artifacts**
- 18 artifacts, crafted dormant and woken by drawing their glyph, traced from a faint guide.
- Washing Barrel, Raincleaver, Glowstone Path, Arcane Lens, Dragon Teacup, Sealchair, Beast Warding Tower, Snugstone, Vapor Bubble, Mirror Mantle, Wingcloak, Tent Staff, Light Tracer Bangle, Capture Pennant and the Void Basin, which drops you into a world of stars.
- Doorknob Doors: a bound pair of brass knobs; walk through a door wearing one and step out of the door wearing the other. Guidance Orb: seal a fragment inside and a thread of light points to where it came from. Fluttergram: a paper bird that flies a letter to a player by name. All three come from the manga; the details are the mod's own: knobs lead only from their own side and work across dimensions, the orb's rules (a block finds its kind, raw ore its ore, a creature's drop the creature, a forbidden page a Brimhat, a grimoire its keeper), and a fluttergram's delay, its letters-only cargo and its flight home after a day with no one to find.
- Sylph shoes: draw the flight glyph on your boots, click your heels and fly until water washes it off.
- A witch's outfit: hat, mantle, robe and boots.

**The Pact**
- Brimhats cast real glyphs; the Stranger trades inks, pens and Silverwood.
- Forbidden magic, and magic made where villagers can see it, draw the Knights Moralis, who keep hunting until the debt is paid.
- A villager who sees magic made runs for the village bell; a Seal of Obliviation takes the sighting back. Knights try to take a witch alive and erase instead of kill.
- What forbidden magic does, only forbidden ink undoes: a petrified creature wakes fully only to a restoring glyph in blood ink.

**The world**
- The Passage Stars cross the night sky now and then, and the harvest after them comes early.
- Advancements for the things a witch learns the hard way.

**Compatibility**
- Create Aeronautics: a levitation glyph on a ship's underside flies the ship.
- EMI and JEI: recipes, info pages and an Awakening category with each artifact's glyph.

## Build

```bash
./gradlew build
```

Run the client with `./gradlew runClient`, tests with `./gradlew test` and `./gradlew runGameTestServer`.
