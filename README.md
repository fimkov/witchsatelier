# Witch's Atelier

Draw your spells. A magic mod for Minecraft inspired by Witch Hat Atelier: ink a sigil, signs around it, close the ring.

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · [Wiki](https://fimkov.github.io/witchsatelier-wiki/)

## Features

**Drawing magic**
- Draw glyphs by hand on any block face (floors, walls, ceilings, across several blocks), on paper, in a Palm Quire, on boots and on artifacts.
- The spell wakes the moment the ring closes, and stops when the ring is broken, rubbed out or the block under it is gone.
- 8 sigils (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light, Crystal, Wind Underfoot, Aeriforms) and 25 signs; reversed signs give the opposite effect.
- How it is drawn matters: a bigger ring makes a bigger, stronger spell, a lopsided layout bends it, a careless hand misfires.
- 51 grimoire spells, from Pillar of Flame and Wall Breaker to Windowway portals, Birds of Light, a Water Horse, a Wall of Wind and a floating Pyreball that cooks your supper.
- A quick-close key for prepared pages and an imagined-ring technique.

**Spellbuilding**
- Rings inside rings, as deep as you like: every band adds its own layer to the spell at the heart.
- Sigils beside the middle one mix their elements in: Floating Drops, Rainflinger, Beast Repellent. Like the old books draw them, they sit in small circles in lunes cut from the rim, and the middle sigil can have a ring of its own.
- Big signs make a strong spell, small ones a weak one; a longer sign on one side pulls the spell that way.
- Region signs say where a spell may happen: inside the ring, outside it, on its line, or to one side.
- A straight line struck through a sign switches it off; Columns turned inward lay the element flat.
- A Tread glyph closed while someone stands in it answers only to them; turned inward, it works the other way round.
- Glyphs joined by lines are one chain: they light in a wave, or wait for a Tread glyph that works while it is stood on or powered by redstone: doors, traps, lights on a lever.
- Glyph arrows cast where they strike; dispensers shoot them, or cast a page's glyph from their own front.
- A Witch's Grimoire of your own: draw, name and annotate your glyphs, copy them from walls and old books, copy the whole book for a friend.
- Waymarked Windowways: signs drawn around a Window name a place, and a page with a mark on it, held up before any marked window, turns that window toward the mark's home. A network of portals with no menus, across the world and between dimensions (see below).

**Glyphs that last**
- Continuous spells work for as long as the glyph is whole.
- Rain washes ordinary ink and feet scuff glyphs on the floor; the ink thins, runs and smears before it breaks.
- Repetition keeps a glyph mended.

**Inks and tools**
- Conjuring, waterproof, invisible and lasting inks brewed from Silverwood, plus forbidden blood ink.
- Ink Pen and inkwell, Woodcruor Wand (chalk on stone), Searneedle (burns into metal), a rag to erase.
- Silverwood trees, and library ruins with themed grimoires, loose glyph pages and rarer finds.

**Artifacts**
- 18 artifacts, crafted dormant and woken by drawing their glyph, traced from a faint guide.
- Washing Barrel, Raincleaver, Glowstone Path, Arcane Lens, Dragon Teacup, Sealchair, Beast Warding Tower, Snugstone, Vapor Bubble, Mirror Mantle, Wingcloak, Tent Staff, Light Tracer Bangle, Capture Pennant and the Void Basin, which drops you into a world of stars.
- Doorknob Doors: a bound pair of brass knobs; walk through a door wearing one and step out of the door wearing the other. Guidance Orb: seal a fragment inside and a thread of light points to where it came from. Fluttergram: a paper bird that flies a letter to a player by name. All three come from the manga; the details are the mod's own: knobs lead only from their own side and work across dimensions, the orb's rules (a block finds its kind, raw ore its ore, a creature's drop the creature, a forbidden page a Brimhat, a grimoire its keeper), and a fluttergram's delay, its letters-only cargo and its flight home after a day with no one to find.
- Sylph shoes: draw the flight glyph on your boots, click your heels and fly until water washes it off.
- A witch's outfit: hat, mantle, robe and boots.

**The Pact**
- Brimhats cast real glyphs; the Stranger trades inks, pens and Silverwood.
- Forbidden magic, and magic made where villagers can see it, draw the Knights Moralis, who keep hunting until the debt is paid.
- A villager who sees magic made runs for the village bell; a Seal of Obliviation takes the sighting back. Knights try to take a witch alive and erase instead of kill.
- What forbidden magic does, only forbidden ink undoes: a petrified creature wakes fully only to a restoring glyph in blood ink.

**The world**
- The Passage Stars cross the night sky now and then, and the harvest after them comes early.
- Advancements for the things a witch learns the hard way.

**Compatibility**
- Create Aeronautics: a levitation glyph on a ship's underside flies the ship.
- EMI and JEI: recipes, info pages and an Awakening category with each artifact's glyph.

## Waymarked Windowways

A plain Windowway (the Window sign alone in a closed ring) joins the next plain Window its witch draws, as it always has. Draw two or more signs around the Window as well and it bears a **mark**, and the window joins a network.

- **The mark** is the name of a place. Only which signs are drawn, and how many, make the name: where they sit on the ring, which way they face, how big they are and the order they were drawn in do not. A Window with a single extra sign, or a sign the ink cannot make out, stays a plain Window. Tread, Diamond, Crosshair, Weave, Collection, Float and Dancing Puppet keep their usual effect on the window and are no part of the name. Each mark shows its own rim colour, the same on every server and after every restart.
- **Sill.** The first window drawn with a mark is that mark's Sill: the place the mark lives. Its witch holds the mark from then on.
- **Kin.** The Sill's witch can draw a second window with the same mark. It becomes the Kin, and Sill and Kin stand open to each other both ways, like a plain pair.
- **Visitor.** Every other window with the mark, drawn by anyone else, a third one by the same witch, or cast from a page, is a Visitor: it opens one way, onto the Sill. A Visitor drawn by another player opens only once that player has discovered the Sill (see below), so knowing a mark is not enough to reach someone's place. A hall of Visitors works as a station hall. Pick signs of your own for your marks: the grimoire's example is the first one everybody tries.
- **Held pages.** Copy a Sill into a Witch's Grimoire (sneak and use on the glyph) or draw the whole window on paper (the ring, the Window and its mark signs; the signs alone are not enough), and title the page yourself. Stand up to 4 blocks in front of any marked window holding the page in either hand, and within a quarter of a second the window closes like an iris and reopens onto the Sill of the page's mark: a live picture when it is close, a fog in the mark's colour when it is far or in another dimension. Walk through; companions who cross within 3 seconds follow. The window turns back to its own link half a second after you step away, or 3 seconds after the last crossing. While one player holds a window, nobody else can turn it.
- **The way back.** A Sill you arrive at that has no link of its own opens back toward the window you came from for 10 seconds, so you can step back without a page.
- **Discovery.** Another player's Sill answers your page, and opens to the Visitors you draw with its mark, only once you have stood within 6 blocks of it for 2 seconds, or come out of it. Your own Sills always answer.
- **Blood.** A Sill drawn mostly in blood ink lets only its own witch arrive; anyone else is turned away, and sees the windows that look at it as shut. Tracing over a lit Sill in blood, or adding plain ink to a blood one, changes this at once.
- **Tread.** A Tread sign in the ring keeps a marked window shut until it is stood in or powered, and a shut Sill accepts no arrivals.
- **Arrivals are checked.** If there is no room to stand in front of the Sill, even a block or two further out or one up (solid blocks, water, lava or fire), the crossing is refused. Players, items and creatures cross; the Ender Dragon, the Wither, the Warden (the `witchsatelier:windowway_blocked` entity tag) and anything carrying a rider do not.
- **Refusals** of any kind (no Sill, not discovered, private, shut, blocked exit, another dimension when that is off) are a thunk and a dim flicker of the disc. There is never any text.
- **Erasing.** Wiping a Sill away passes its mark to its witch's oldest remaining window with that mark, or else to the oldest window anyone has with it. A restart or an unloaded chunk changes nothing: windows keep their roles, links and colours, and a far Sill is reached even while nobody is near it.
- **Limits:** 64 marked windows per player and 4096 per server; a window over the limit shows a quiet rim and does nothing.
- **Gamerules:** `witchsatelierWindowwaysAcrossDimensions` (default true) lets a marked window open onto a Sill in another dimension; `witchsatelierWindowwayDiscovery` (default true) makes other players' Sills answer pages and Visitors only after they have been discovered.

The grimoire *Of Uncommon Signs* teaches it in two pages, "Waymarks" and "A Page Held Up".

## Build

```bash
./gradlew build
```

Run the client with `./gradlew runClient`, tests with `./gradlew test` and `./gradlew runGameTestServer`.
